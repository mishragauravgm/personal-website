## Northeastern University AI Tech Associate
**January 2022 - December 2023** | *Boston, MA*

### The LLM Revolution (Before It Was Cool!)
This was back when ChatGPT was just a whisper in tech circles, and I was already fine-tuning open-source Large Language Models! Using **HuggingFace**, **LangChain**, and **FAISS**, I built a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system that could provide contextual, factual information based on our organization's data.

### The Anti-Hallucination Crusade
One of my proudest achievements was significantly reducing LLM hallucinations - those moments when AI confidently states completely made-up facts. By implementing RAG through careful prompt engineering and vector databases, I created a system that stayed grounded in reality while still being creative and helpful.

### Data-Driven Decisions
I spearheaded A/B testing strategies using **Python**, **Flask**, **FastAPI**, and **Tableau** to optimize everything. We increased operational efficiency by approximately 50%.

### The Tech Stack Adventures
Working with **JIRA** and **SQL** for project management and data analysis while building REST API endpoints on **AWS** was like conducting a tech orchestra. Each tool had its part to play in creating a symphony of efficiency and innovation..
