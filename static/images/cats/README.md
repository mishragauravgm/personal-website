# Cat Pictures 🐱

This folder is for your cat pictures and photos of your foster cats.

## How to use these images on your website:

Once you upload your cat pictures here, you can reference them in your content files using:

```markdown
![Cat name](../images/cats/your-cat-photo.jpg)
```

## Suggested image formats:
- JPG or PNG
- Recommended size: 800px-1200px wide for good quality
- Keep file sizes under 2MB for faster loading

## Photo ideas:
- Your two fur babies
- Foster cats you've helped
- Cute moments and funny poses
- Before/after foster transformations
- Cats helping with your coding (walking on keyboard!) 😸

Happy uploading! 🐾
