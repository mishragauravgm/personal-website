# Cooking & Food Pictures 🍳

This folder is for your cooking adventures and delicious dishes.

## How to use these images on your website:

Once you upload your cooking pictures here, you can reference them in your content files using:

```markdown
![Dish name](../images/cooking/your-dish-photo.jpg)
```

## Suggested image formats:
- JPG or PNG
- Recommended size: 800px-1200px wide for good quality
- Keep file sizes under 2MB for faster loading

## Photo ideas:
- Your fusion dishes (Indian + international cuisine)
- Cooking process shots
- Beautifully plated final dishes
- Ingredients and spices
- Kitchen experiments and creations
- Meals you've shared with friends/family

Show off your culinary creativity! 👨‍🍳
