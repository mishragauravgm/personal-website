# Hiking & Outdoor Adventure Pictures 🏔️

This folder is for your outdoor adventures and hiking photos.

## How to use these images on your website:

Once you upload your hiking pictures here, you can reference them in your content files using:

```markdown
![Location/Adventure name](../images/hikes/your-hike-photo.jpg)
```

## Suggested image formats:
- JPG or PNG
- Recommended size: 800px-1200px wide for good quality
- Keep file sizes under 2MB for faster loading

## Photo ideas:
- White Mountains hiking adventures
- Deerfield River tubing
- Beautiful landscape shots
- Mountain peaks and trails
- Nature photography
- Action shots during activities
- Scenic viewpoints and vistas
- Wildlife encounters

Capture the beauty of New England outdoors! 🥾
